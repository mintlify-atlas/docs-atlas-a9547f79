JyutTranslate is a full-stack Progressive Web App for real-time Cantonese ↔ English translation. Speak into your microphone, point your camera at text, or upload a document — JyutTranslate handles the rest, complete with Jyutping romanization, tone contour letters, and character-level drill-downs. This documentation covers local development setup, core feature details, plan metering, self-hosting configuration, and the full REST API.
