JyutTranslate offers four plan tiers designed to scale from a quick no-account trial all the way up to a ten-seat Business deployment. Each tier unlocks a different set of monthly usage meters — live microphone minutes, tap-to-play TTS characters, camera scan credits, and document pages — while higher tiers add Auto-speak, AI vision OCR fallbacks, and pooled household seats.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Guest | Free | Family | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sign-in required** | No (cookie trial) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Seats** | 1 | 1 | 4 | 10 |
| **Live mic** | 30 min / month | 60 min / month | 480 min / month | 2 400 min / month |
| **TTS** | Unlimited (metered) | 30 000 chars / month (hard cap) | Unlimited (metered) | Unlimited (metered) |
| **Camera scans** | 30 scans / month | 120 scans / month | 800 scans / month | Unlimited (metered) |
| **Document pages** | ✗ (sign-in required) | 40 pages / month | 400 pages / month | Unlimited (metered) |
| **Auto-speak** | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **AI vision OCR fallbacks** | ✗ | 200 / month | 2 000 / month | 10 000 / month |
| **Text translate + Jyutping** | ✓ unlimited | ✓ unlimited | ✓ unlimited | ✓ unlimited |

<Note>
  "Unlimited (metered)" means there is no hard cap that blocks access, but usage is still recorded each month. Family and Business TTS counters appear in the Admin Users table for capacity planning. The Free plan's 30 000-character TTS cap is a hard gate — requests are blocked once it is exhausted.
</Note>

## Guest — cookie trial

Guest visitors receive an HttpOnly `yue_guest_id` cookie (1 year, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on HTTPS) and can try live translation and camera scans without creating an account. The trial is governed by the `YUE_GUEST_LIVE_MINUTES` and `YUE_GUEST_CAMERA_SCANS` environment variables — both default to **30** in production.

When either trial meter is exhausted, the UI prompts the visitor to sign in and continue on the Free plan. Usage accumulated during the guest session is merged into the new user account on sign-in using cookie, device, and network identity candidates.

<Info>
  A full site-data clear **and** a new IP still yields a fresh guest trial. A shared café NAT shares one network trial — this is intentional behaviour designed to minimise abuse.
</Info>

## Free — signed in

Free accounts get 60 live minutes, 30 000 TTS characters (hard cap), 120 camera scans, and 40 document pages per calendar month. Text translate and Jyutping breakdown remain unlimited for all signed-in users. Auto-speak playback is not available on Free; the preference can be set in Account Hub but is gated at runtime.

## Family — 4 seats

The Family plan provides 480 live minutes (≈ 8 hours), 800 camera scans, 400 document pages, and unlimited TTS per month across a shared household of up to 4 seats. Auto-speak is enabled for all members. AI vision OCR fallbacks are capped at 2 000 per month as an abuse brake. Monthly meters pool on `household_usage_months` so every member draws from a single shared allowance.

See [Household Seats and Pooled Usage](/plans/household) for full seat management details.

## Business — 10 seats

Business expands the household to 10 seats with 2 400 live minutes, unlimited camera scans, unlimited document pages, unlimited TTS, and a 10 000 AI vision OCR cap per month. The `cameraUnlimited` and `docsUnlimited` flags are set to `true` in the entitlement snapshot so no scan or page gate ever fires for Business members.

## Environment variable reference

All plan limits are read from environment variables at startup. The defaults below match the values in `.env.example`; production overrides live in `vercel.json`.

| Variable | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `YUE_OPEN_MODE` | `0` | `1` = Family-like limits with no metering (local dev only). Keep `0` in production. |
| `YUE_REQUIRE_LOGIN` | `1` | When `1`, guests cannot start a live or camera session without signing in. |
| `YUE_GUEST_LIVE_MINUTES` | `30` | Guest trial live minutes per month (production). Set to `0` in `.env.example` for local dev. |
| `YUE_GUEST_CAMERA_SCANS` | `30` | Guest trial camera scan credits per month. |
| `YUE_FREE_LIVE_MINUTES` | `60` | Free plan live minutes. |
| `YUE_FREE_TTS_CHARS` | `30000` | Free plan TTS character cap (hard gate). |
| `YUE_FREE_CAMERA_SCANS` | `120` | Free plan camera scan credits. |
| `YUE_FREE_DOCS_PAGES` | `40` | Free plan document pages. |
| `YUE_FREE_AI_VISION_COUNT` | `200` | Free plan AI vision OCR fallback cap. |
| `YUE_FREE_ALLOW_LIVE` | `1` | Enable live mic for Free plan (`0` to disable). |
| `YUE_FREE_ALLOW_TTS` | `1` | Enable tap-to-play TTS for Free plan. |
| `YUE_FREE_ALLOW_CAMERA` | `1` | Enable camera scans and documents for Free plan. |
| `YUE_FAMILY_LIVE_MINUTES` | `480` | Family plan live minutes. |
| `YUE_FAMILY_CAMERA_SCANS` | `800` | Family plan camera scan credits. |
| `YUE_FAMILY_DOCS_PAGES` | `400` | Family plan document pages. |
| `YUE_FAMILY_AI_VISION_COUNT` | `2000` | Family plan AI vision OCR fallback cap. |
| `YUE_BUSINESS_LIVE_MINUTES` | `2400` | Business plan live minutes. |
| `YUE_BUSINESS_AI_VISION_COUNT` | `10000` | Business plan AI vision OCR fallback cap. |
| `YUE_APP_URL` | `http://localhost:5173` | Public app URL used for login/upgrade links, Stripe redirects, and CORS. |

<Tip>
  Legacy env names (`YUE_PRO_*`, `YUE_MAX_*`, `STRIPE_PRICE_PRO_*`, `STRIPE_PRICE_MAX_*`) are still accepted for backwards compatibility with older deployments, but the `YUE_FAMILY_*` and `YUE_BUSINESS_*` names are canonical.
</Tip>

## Upgrading and Stripe Checkout

Clicking an upgrade button in Account Hub redirects to Stripe Checkout. The upgrade URL follows the pattern:

```
{YUE_APP_URL}/#/pricing
```

Stripe Checkout has `allow_promotion_codes` enabled, so users can enter a promotion code at checkout. Monthly and annual price IDs are configured via `STRIPE_PRICE_FAMILY_MONTH`, `STRIPE_PRICE_FAMILY_YEAR`, `STRIPE_PRICE_BUSINESS_MONTH`, and `STRIPE_PRICE_BUSINESS_YEAR` in your server environment. Invite emails are sent via Resend when `RESEND_API_KEY` and `YUE_NOTIFY_FROM` are configured.
